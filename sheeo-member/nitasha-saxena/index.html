Meet The Founder

About Nitasha

Nitasha Saxena is the founder of Mindful Make-Up, a Dubai-based beauty education platform focused on helping teens, tweens, and women develop confidence through age-appropriate makeup and skincare knowledge.

Through interactive workshops and practical beauty education, Nitasha teaches women and young girls how to understand makeup products, ingredient awareness, skincare basics, and techniques that enhance natural beauty without overwhelming trends or unrealistic standards.

Her approach combines beauty, confidence, wellbeing, and education, creating a safe space where women and young girls can learn the right way to care for their skin and express themselves confidently.
