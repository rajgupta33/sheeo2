Meet The Founder

About Rashi

Rashi is the founder of Milagro by Rashi, a Dubai-based lifestyle house built on the belief that the spaces we create, the rituals we practice, and the way we express ourselves have the power to transform how we feel and live.

Through two distinct yet connected brands — Mohaul and Milagro Studio by Rashi — she creates products and experiences that bring intention, beauty, and meaning into everyday life.

Whether it's a handcrafted candle designed to create calm and mindfulness, a bespoke gifting experience created for a special occasion, or a carefully curated fashion piece that inspires confidence, every creation is designed to help women live more intentionally and authentically.
