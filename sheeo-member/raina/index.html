Meet The Founder

About Raina

Raina Desai Lalchand is the founder of Emarkiz Web Solutions, a Dubai-based e-commerce and digital growth company helping businesses establish, scale, and optimize their online presence.

With over five years of experience in digital commerce, marketplace management, and online business growth, Raina works with entrepreneurs, retailers, and brands looking to expand beyond traditional sales channels and unlock new revenue opportunities.

Through Emarkiz, she provides end-to-end e-commerce solutions, helping businesses launch online stores, enter major marketplaces, improve visibility, and scale profitably through strategic digital marketing and marketplace growth initiatives.
