Meet The Founder

About Mehak

Mehak Marwaha is the founder of Ekaa, a purpose-driven ecosystem built around personal growth, transformation, celebration, and holistic wellbeing.

Through her three verticals — Ekaa Radiance, Ekaa Rise, and Ekaa Rejoice — she supports individuals at different stages of their journey, helping them find clarity in education, confidence in life decisions, emotional balance through healing, and joy through meaningful experiences.

As a Holistic Clarity Coach and Career Guidance Expert, Mehak has guided hundreds of students, women, and professionals through challenges related to overthinking, career uncertainty, emotional stress, and life transitions. Her work combines practical guidance with intuitive insights, empowering people to move forward with greater awareness and confidence.
